Word Melt – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Word Melt is a word game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: we don't run any servers or ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. The game uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and Google AdMob to show ads.

Information stored on your device

Your games in progress, scores, personal bests, Daily Challenge progress and streaks, achievements, the words you've found, and your settings (such as sound and appearance) are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center (Apple)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and your achievements are recorded there, under your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center, or change who can see your profile, in your iPhone's Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Melt shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Some are optional: you can choose to watch one for a reward, such as extra time or skipping a level. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when asked;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (UK and EEA): if you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings › Privacy › Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Tracking: the game asks for Apple's permission to track. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but aren't personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Personalised ads: you can limit personalised advertising across apps in your iPhone's Settings.

Children

Word Melt is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
